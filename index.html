About FPAA

Connecting the past. Empowering the present. Building the future.

The Falakata Polytechnic Alumni Association (FPAA) is a united community of former students of Falakata Polytechnic, connected by a shared institution, cherished memories, lifelong friendships and a commitment to giving back.

FPAA was established to build a strong, inclusive, transparent and sustainable alumni network that brings together alumni, students, faculty, the institution and the wider community, through networking, knowledge sharing, mentorship, career support and social initiatives.

FPAA Connect 2.0 is the digital extension of this vision: one place for alumni to stay connected, discover opportunities, take part in association activities and stay close to their alma mater.

Once a Falakata Polytechnician, always a family.

Our roots. Our people. Our pride.
Our vision
To build a connected, empowered and globally recognised Falakata Polytechnic alumni community that contributes meaningfully to the growth of its members, students and institution.
We envision a network where
  • Every alumnus stays meaningfully connected with the institute.
  • Alumni from every batch, department and profession can easily connect.
  • Students benefit from alumni guidance and professional networks.
  • Knowledge and skills are shared for collective development.
  • The legacy of Falakata Polytechnic is preserved and its future strengthened.
Alumni association goals

Our 12 strategic goals

  • 01

    Build a strong alumni network

    An organised, growing network connecting alumni across batches, departments, professions, locations and generations.

  • 02

    Strengthen alumni–institution relations

    A continuous channel between alumni and Falakata Polytechnic for mutual development and institutional progress.

  • 03

    Support current students

    Mentorship, career guidance, internships, industrial exposure and skill development for students.

  • 04

    Promote professional networking

    Connect alumni by profession, industry, expertise and interest for collaboration and opportunities.

  • 05

    Create a knowledge-sharing community

    Share experience, technical knowledge, industry insight and learning resources with students and fellow alumni.

  • 06

    Encourage career and employment

    Share jobs, internships, recruitment information and career guidance within the community.

  • 07

    Encourage entrepreneurship

    Support alumni entrepreneurs through collaboration, business networking and partnerships.

  • 08

    Preserve institutional heritage

    Protect and celebrate the history, memories, achievements and traditions of Falakata Polytechnic.

  • 09

    Organise meaningful activities

    Reunions, seminars, workshops, mentorship, sports, cultural and social programmes.

  • 10

    Stay transparent and inclusive

    Responsible administration, equal participation and clear communication for every alumnus.

  • 11

    Support the institution's development

    Collaborate on infrastructure, academics, student welfare, technical education and industry interaction.

  • 12

    Build a digital alumni ecosystem

    Through FPAA Connect 2.0: registration, networking, events, announcements and association management online.

Savitribai Fule Excellence Scholarship

সাবিত্রীবাই ফুলে উৎকর্ষ বৃত্তি

Financial help for meritorious, financially disadvantaged female students in 2nd year of a full-time diploma at Falakata Polytechnic.

₹12,000 / year75% meritIncome ≤ ₹1.75 lakhFemale students
Eligibility, documents and selection
Eligibility
Female students admitted to 2nd year; minimum 75% aggregate in 1st and 2nd semester board exams; gross annual family income up to ₹1,75,000; not receiving another major recurring central or state scholarship.
Benefit
₹12,000 per year, extendable to the end of 3rd year on renewal. First-year amount paid in two half-yearly DBT instalments.
Documents
1st and 2nd semester mark sheets and Class 10/12 certificate; college admission letter and current fee receipt; valid income certificate; proof of identity and domicile; bank passbook or cancelled cheque in the student's name, linked with Aadhaar for DBT.
Selection
Screening for completeness and authenticity, then ranking by academic performance and income. A provisional list is followed by a 10-day grievance period before the final list.
Renewal
At least 70% aggregate in the 4th semester board exam and at least 75% attendance through 2nd year, with updated fee receipts and mark sheets.

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Youth Skill Development Programme

ড. এ. পি. জে. আব্দুল কালাম যুব দক্ষতা উন্নয়ন কর্মসূচি

Professional readiness training that bridges classroom learning and industry expectations through technical skills, soft skills and professional ethics.

Technical + soft skillsProjectsMock interviewsCertificate
Eligibility, evaluation and certification
Eligibility
Open to Falakata Polytechnic students. Minimum 75% attendance across technical, soft-skills and project modules, with active participation and professional conduct.
What you get
Technical and soft-skills training, project exposure, mock interviews, portfolio development and a Professional Readiness Certificate.
Evaluation
Continuous assessment through practical assignments, project milestones, quizzes and peer evaluation.
Certification
85% cumulative attendance, completed assignments and capstone with at least 60% aggregate, good conduct, mock interviews and portfolio submission.
